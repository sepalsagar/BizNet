# BizNet — Polished Vertical Launch Reel

**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 15 seconds  
**Tone:** polished — restrained motion, deep navy canvas, white typography, precise electric-blue accents  
**Music:** 120 BPM minimal electronic pulse; every scene change lands on a beat.

## Angle

BizNet gives a small business one clear operational view: what is selling, what needs attention, and what to do next. The reel treats its dashboard like a calm command center rather than a feature checklist.

## Audience

Small and medium business operators who want sales, stock, purchasing, customers, analytics, pricing experiments, and AI guidance in one workspace.

## Visual Identity

Use the real BizNet UI: Plus Jakarta Sans, slate surfaces, white cards, blue action accents, real dashboard and analytics views. App captures sit as crisp floating panels on a deep slate background with roomy typography.
