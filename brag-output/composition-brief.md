# Hyperframes Composition Brief: BizNet

## Objective

Create a 20-second polished vertical launch reel for BizNet using close, full-frame crops of the actual product UI.

## Output

- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: vertical — 1080x1920
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Creative Direction

- Tone preset: polished
- Hook: "The next move is already in your numbers."
- Avoid: floating mini screenshots, generic SaaS claims, empty background space, and mojibake.

## Visual Identity

- Background: #0f172a
- Accent: #4f46e5
- Display / body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Visual references: real dashboard KPIs, analytics graph, pricing simulator, and AI Assistant UI.

## Storyboard

1. Dashboard KPI crop — 4.4 seconds — counters and KPI focus at beat-grid times.
2. Analytics crop — 4.7 seconds — chart and performance signals fill the frame.
3. Pricing Simulator crop — 4.6 seconds — simulated control adjustment and outcome focus.
4. AI Assistant crop — 3.8 seconds — prompt, response, and contextual guidance.
5. Dashboard outro — 2.5 seconds — BizNet wordmark and URL over a readable UI crop.

## Audio

- Use bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.34 volume and fade during the final second.
- Major cue locks: 8.74s and 13.11s. KPI detail arrivals: 3.27s, 3.82s, and 4.39s.
- Use sparse soft UI click, control tap, and logo resolve SFX.
- Subtle card/halo breathing may react to music; no waveform visuals.
